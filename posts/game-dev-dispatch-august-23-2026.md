The major game engines continue to push boundaries, offering developers more robust tools and features.

Unity has rolled out a new minor update, focusing on performance optimizations and workflow enhancements for mobile and XR development. Key improvements include:

*   **Enhanced Mobile Performance:** Significant gains in rendering efficiency for iOS and Android platforms, allowing for more complex visuals on a wider range of devices.

Unreal Engine continues its stride with updates focused on photorealism and developer productivity. This week's highlights include:

*   **Lumen and Nanite Enhancements:** Further refinements to Lumen global illumination and Nanite virtualized geometry, enabling even more realistic lighting and incredibly detailed environments with optimized performance.
