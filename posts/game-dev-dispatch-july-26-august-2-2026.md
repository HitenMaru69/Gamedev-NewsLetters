# Game Dev Dispatch: Your Weekly Dose of Industry Insights

Welcome to this week's Game Dev Dispatch, your essential roundup of the most significant happenings in game development over the past seven days. From major engine announcements to shifting industry landscapes and captivating indie showcases, we've got you covered.

## Major Industry News & Breakthroughs

### Sony's All-Digital Future Sparks Gamer Outcry

Sony has announced plans to cease production of physical discs for all first-party and third-party PlayStation games starting January 2028. While games released before this date will still have physical versions, all new titles thereafter will be digital-only. This move has ignited significant backlash from gamers, with some calling for a worldwide boycott of Sony platforms in late August and a Change.org petition titled "Don't Kill the Disc" garnering over 350,000 signatures. The shift is driven by consumer trends and a preference for digital media, but it raises concerns about the future of the second-hand game market, estimated at nearly $7 billion, which is expected to shrink and eventually disappear.

### Ongoing Layoffs Continue to Impact the Industry

The game industry continues to face a challenging period with projected layoffs for 2026 increasing to 14,259, a 78% rise from the initial January forecast. Confirmed layoffs through July 26, 2026, reached 9,781, indicating an accelerating pace of job cuts. Microsoft's Xbox division announced 4,800 job cuts in early July, with 3,200 within its gaming division this year and next, leading to a significant restructuring. This restructuring includes beloved indie-focused studios like Compulsion Games and Double Fine Productions transitioning back to independence, and Ninja Theory and Undead Labs moving under new ownership.

### EA Acquisition Nears Completion

The acquisition of Electronic Arts (EA) by a consortium of investors led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund is set to close on August 4, 2026, following regulatory approvals. This $55 billion deal marks a significant consolidation in the gaming industry.

### NVIDIA and SEGA Celebrate 30 Years of Collaboration

NVIDIA and SEGA are celebrating over three decades of partnership by bringing *Virtua Fighter Crossroads* and future SEGA titles to NVIDIA RTX Spark, a new superchip for slim Windows laptops and compact desktop PCs. This collaboration aims to offer gamers new experiences leveraging NVIDIA's ray tracing, DLSS, and AI technologies.

## Software Engine Updates

### Unity Unveils Unity 7 with "Zero Rebuilding" Promise

Unity Technologies announced Unity 7 at Unite Seoul, promising a seamless upgrade path from Unity 6 with "zero rebuilding" of projects. This next-generation engine, expected to enter early beta in December 2026 and fully release in early 2027, aims for speedier workflows, an open software ecosystem, improved graphics, and "smarter growth and monetization". Key features include up to 90% faster shader compile times, real-time global illumination via Surface Cache GI, and a public CLI for non-editor collaboration. Unity is also integrating AI-assisted graphics optimization and Unity Vector, while emphasizing that developers will have control over the extent of generative AI use in their projects.
