### Godot Engine

Godot users had a busy week with several important updates:

*   A new **development snapshot, Godot 4.8 dev 4**, was released on August 26, 2026, indicating active progress on the next major iteration.
*   A crucial fix addressing **high polling rate mice performance issues on Windows** in Godot was detailed on August 24, 2026, a welcome improvement for many developers.
*   The **Godot 3.6.3 maintenance release** was made available on August 22, 2026, providing continued stability and addressing changes in requirements for Android and iOS.
*   Submissions are currently open for the **Godot 2026 showreel**, inviting developers to showcase their published or in-progress Godot creations.
